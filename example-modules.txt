**Level:** A1 to B1. Each module works on its own.
**Focus:** Spoken Finnish (*puhekieli*), the way people actually talk: *mä / sä*, *onks*, *mennään*.

**Every lesson follows the same rhythm (about 45 min):**
1. **Warm-up.** One real situation, one question: "What would you say?"
2. **Key phrases.** 8–12 chunks you can use whole, no grammar required.
3. **Listen.** A short dialogue, first slowed down, then at real speed.
4. **Role-play.** You practise, then switch roles.
5. **Rescue move.** One phrase that gets you out of trouble in that situation.

---

## 00 · 🔑 Start here: How Finns actually talk
*The bridge between textbook Finnish and the street.*

- **0.1 Book Finnish vs spoken Finnish.** *minä → mä, sinä → sä, olen → oon*
- **0.2 The five sounds that trip everyone.** Long vowels, double consonants, *y / ä / ö*
- **0.3 Questions without the textbook -ko.** *Onks? Tuuks? Meneeks?*
- **0.4 Your survival kit.** *Anteeks, mitä? Voitsä puhuu hitaammin? Mitä se tarkottaa?*

## 01 · 👋 Small talk with new people
*How to open, keep going, and not freeze on question two.*

- **1.1 Hi and names.** *Moi! Mä oon Duc. Mikä sun nimi on?*
- **1.2 Where you're from.** *Mistä sä oot kotoisin? Kauanks sä oot asunu Suomessa?*
- **1.3 Work and everyday life.** *Mitä sä teet työksesi?*
- **1.4 Keeping it going.** Reactions and follow-ups: *Ai jaa! Oikeesti? Entä sä?*
- **1.5 Weather and weekends,** the national small-talk topics. *Onpa kylmä! Mitä teit viikonloppuna?*
- **1.6 Rescue move:** switching languages gracefully without ending the conversation.

## 02 · 🍽️ Ordering and chatting at a restaurant
*Beyond "yksi kahvi, kiitos": handling the follow-up questions.*

- **2.1 Ordering naturally.** *Mä ottaisin… Saisinko…*
- **2.2 The follow-ups you didn't expect.** *Täällä vai mukaan? Tuleeko muuta? Kuitti mukaan?*
- **2.3 Diets and allergies.** *Onks tässä laktoosia? Mä en syö lihaa.*
- **2.4 Asking for things and fixing mistakes.** *Voisinko saada vettä? Tää ei oo se mitä tilasin.*
- **2.5 Paying and splitting.** *Maksetaanko erikseen? Kortilla, kiitos.*
- **2.6 Lunch with friends.** Talking about the food: *Tää on tosi hyvää!*

## 03 · 🏫 Daycare and school conversations
*Quick exchanges at pickup and parent chats.*

- **3.1 Drop-off and pickup.** *Miten päivä meni? Nukkuiko se?*
- **3.2 Understanding the teacher's quick report,** at real speed.
- **3.3 Sick days and absences.** *Se on tänään kotona kipeenä.*
- **3.4 Chatting with other parents** at the gate.
- **3.5 The parent meeting** (*vasukeskustelu*): goals, worries, questions.
- **3.6 Reading and replying to Wilma and daycare messages.**

## 04 · 🏥 At the doctor
*Describing what's wrong and understanding the answer.*

- **4.1 Booking by phone.** *Haluaisin varata ajan lääkärille.*
- **4.2 Symptoms.** *Mulla on kuumetta. Päätä särkee. Kurkku on kipee.*
- **4.3 Body parts and pain.** *Tähän sattuu. Milloin se alko?*
- **4.4 Understanding instructions.** Doses, times, "come back if…"
- **4.5 At the pharmacy.** *Onks tähän jotain ilman reseptiä?*
- **4.6 With your child at the doctor.**

## 05 · 🏡 Planning life at home
*Everyday back-and-forth with a Finnish-speaking partner.*

- **5.1 Daily plans.** *Mitä tänään syödään? Kuka hakee lapset?*
- **5.2 Chores and favours.** *Voitsä viedä roskat?*
- **5.3 Shopping lists and errands.**
- **5.4 Weekend and holiday plans.** Mökki, travel, visiting family.
- **5.5 Disagreeing kindly and apologising.** *Sori, en tarkottanu sitä niin.*
- **5.6 Affection and feelings.** The words Finns use sparingly, but mean.
- **5.7 Visiting the in-laws.** Polite, warm, and still yourself.

## 06 · 🎉 Parties and social events
*Staying in the conversation when it gets fast and loud.*

- **6.1 Arriving.** *Kiitos kutsusta! Mä toin tällasen.*
- **6.2 Joining a group** without interrupting.
- **6.3 Surviving fast speech.** Catch the topic, not every word.
- **6.4 Toasts, birthdays and celebrations.** *Kippis! Paljon onnea!*
- **6.5 Sauna talk.** *Heitetäänkö löylyä? Onks liian kuuma?*
- **6.6 Leaving well.** *Mun pitää lähtee. Kiitos illasta!*

## 07 · 💪 At the gym
*Small talk that doesn't require a dictionary.*

- **7.1 Front desk and membership questions.**
- **7.2 Sharing equipment.** *Onks tää vapaa? Voidaanko vuorotella?*
- **7.3 Following a group class.** *Oikee, vasen, ylös, alas, vielä kerran!*
- **7.4 Chatting with regulars.** *Mitä sä treenaat?*
- **7.5 Talking to a trainer** about goals and injuries.

## 08 · 🤝 Playdates and family logistics
*The conversations that come up again and again.*

- **8.1 Arranging a playdate.** *Pääseekö Emma leikkimään lauantaina?*
- **8.2 Times, places and pickups.** *Mä haen sen kuudelta.*
- **8.3 Allergies, rules and "what does your kid eat?"**
- **8.4 Birthday parties** (*synttärit*): invites, RSVPs, gifts.
- **8.5 Hobbies and carpooling.** *Voisitsä heittää meidän pojan treeneihin?*
- **8.6 The parents' WhatsApp group.** Reading it fast and replying short.

## 09 · 🛒 Errands and services
*Shops, post, bank: the small exchanges that add up.*

- **9.1 At the shop.** *Onks teillä…? Missä on…?*
- **9.2 Returns and exchanges.** *Voinko vaihtaa tän?*
- **9.3 Post and parcels.**
- **9.4 Phone calls to services.** Menus, queues, spelling your name.
- **9.5 Hairdresser and repairs.** Explaining what you want.

## 10 · 🏢 Neighbours and housing
*Living in a taloyhtiö without awkward silences.*

- **10.1 Stairwell greetings** and when Finns don't greet.
- **10.2 Sauna turns, laundry room, and shared spaces.**
- **10.3 Reporting a problem** to maintenance. *Meillä vuotaa hana.*
- **10.4 Talking to the neighbour** about noise, parking and small favours.

## 11 · 💼 Business Finnish
*Working in a Finnish team or with Finnish clients, even when the meeting itself is in English.*

- **11.1 Coffee-room talk.** *Mennäänkö kahville? Miten viikonloppu meni?*
- **11.2 Introducing yourself and your company.** *Me autetaan yrityksiä…*
- **11.3 Meetings.** Opening, agreeing, disagreeing politely. *Mä näen tän vähän eri tavalla.*
- **11.4 Following a meeting** that switches into Finnish: catch decisions and action items.
- **11.5 Scheduling and follow-ups.** *Sovitaanko palaveri ensi viikolle? Palataan tähän.*
- **11.6 Emails and Slack.** Short, direct, the Finnish tone (no fluff).
- **11.7 Networking at events.** Opening lines, business cards, *Pidetään yhteyttä!*
- **11.8 Sales and client calls.** Needs, budget, timelines. *Mikä teille on tärkeintä?*
- **11.9 Negotiating and saying no.** Finnish directness without sounding rude.
- **11.10 Rescue move:** *Voidaanko jatkaa englanniksi?* Also its better version, *Mä puhun suomea vielä vähän huonosti, mut yritetään!*
