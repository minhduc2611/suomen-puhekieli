# Puhu suomee — Spoken Finnish Conversation Course

**Level:** A1 → B1. Modules are self-contained; take them in any order after Module 00.
**Focus:** *puhekieli* — how Finns actually talk. `mä / sä`, `onks`, `mennään`, `tuu`, `-nut → -nu`.
**Not:** academic/kirjakieli Finnish, grammar tables, case drills.

## Lesson anatomy (every lesson, ~45 min)
1. **Warm-up** — one real situation, one question: "What would you say?"
2. **Vocab** — 12–20 words, each with kirjakieli ↔ puhekieli form, literal gloss, TTS audio
3. **Key phrases** — 8–12 whole chunks, no grammar required, with a "when to use it" note
4. **Dialogue** — 2 dialogues: one slow/clear, one at real speed with elisions
5. **Role-play** — you take one side, then switch
6. **Rescue move** — the one phrase that saves you in this situation

## 00 · 🔑 Start here: how Finns actually talk
*The bridge between textbook Finnish and the street.*
- 0.1 Book Finnish vs spoken Finnish — `minä→mä`, `sinä→sä`, `olen→oon`, `me menemme→mennään`
- 0.2 The five sounds that trip everyone — long vowels, double consonants, `y / ä / ö`
- 0.3 Questions without the textbook `-ko` — `Onks? Tuuks? Meneeks? Oliks?`
- 0.4 Your survival kit — `Anteeks, mitä? Voitsä puhuu hitaammin? Mitä se tarkottaa?`
- 0.5 The sounds Finns make instead of words — `Nii. Joo-o. Öö. No niin. Jaa-a.`
